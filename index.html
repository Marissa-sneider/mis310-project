THE IMPORTANCE OF VISUAL DESIGN

Why does it matter?

Movies and video games are about more than just their stories. The way they look can completely change how we experience them. Colors, lighting, environments, and art styles all play a part in creating emotions and making certain moments memorable.

Visual design helps bring stories to life. It can make a place feel welcoming, create tension during a scary moment, or make a fictional world feel like somewhere you could actually exist. Sometimes, you might not even notice these details at first, but they can have a big impact on how you feel while watching a movie or playing a game.
